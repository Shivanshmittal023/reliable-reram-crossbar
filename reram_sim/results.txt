Points to flag in the report
----------------------------
1. Matching the bit error rate (4.04%) does not give matching accuracy. all_mlc scores
   0.885 under multiplicative noise but 0.57 under cell noise at the matched sigma.
   Additive cell noise hurts small weights far more.
2. Accuracy with no noise and no faults still varies by policy: gradient gets 0.882 on
   DistilBERT and 0.912 on BERT, against digital 0.891 and 0.901. The cause is that
   each tier is quantised per row separately. This gives about +-1 point of baseline
   jitter, roughly 9 of the 872 validation sentences.
